How Counselling Can Help

Understanding your thoughts and feelings

Counselling can provide a safe space to explore thoughts, feelings and experiences that may feel difficult to manage alone.

Managing stress and anxiety

Counselling may help you understand sources of stress and develop ways of coping with difficult situations.

Relationships

Counselling can provide an opportunity to explore communication, boundaries and relationship difficulties.

Personal development

Counselling can provide an opportunity to develop self-awareness, reflect on experiences and work towards personal goals.

Emergency Support

MapYourMind counselling is not an emergency or crisis service.

If you or somebody else is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services by calling 999.

If you require urgent mental-health support, please contact an appropriate urgent mental-health or crisis service in your area.
